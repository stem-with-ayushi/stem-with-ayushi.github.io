Sub-Microscopic Neutralization Engine

Kinetics & Collision Theory

Thermochemical Energy Model

ΔH = -57.1 kJ/mol
  • Bond Breaking is Endothermic (ΔH > 0): Hydration energy is absorbed to disrupt the aqueous solvation shells surrounding H⁺ and OH⁻.
  • Bond Forming is Exothermic (ΔH < 0): When H⁺ and OH⁻ collide with sufficient activation energy (Eₐ), a powerful covalent O—H bond snaps together.
  • Net Enthalpy Release: Because the newly formed covalent bond is significantly stronger than the broken solvation bonds, excess thermal energy is dumped into surrounding water molecules, raising temperature!

Net Ionic Reaction Equation

H⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq) → H₂O(l)

Spectator ions (Na⁺, Cl⁻) do not participate in bond breaking or forming!
