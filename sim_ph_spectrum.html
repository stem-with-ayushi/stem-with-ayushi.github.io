Logarithmic Scale & Biological Systems

10× Ion Gradient
  • pH is Logarithmic: pH = -log₁₀[H⁺]. Every 1-unit shift is a TEN-FOLD (10×) change in free hydrogen ion concentration.
  • Stomach Acid vs Water: pH 1 gastric juice has 1,000,000× more H⁺ ions per cm³ than pH 7 pure water!
  • Human Blood Homeostasis: Human arterial blood is strictly buffered between pH 7.35 and 7.45. A drop below 7.2 causes fatal acidosis.
  • Duodenal Neutralization: The pancreas rapidly neutralizes acidic stomach chyme from pH 1.5 to 8.0 via sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO₃).
