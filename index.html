01 About Me

I am a passionate Physics educator with over 4.5 years of experience teaching diverse curricula at the senior secondary level and in renowned international institutions, including Lancers International School (Gurugram) and The Shriram School, Aravali.

My academic foundation is built on scientific rigor and excellence. I secured Rank 1 in Delhi University during my Bachelor's degree (85%), followed by a Master of Science in Physics from Osmania University (79%) with a specialization in Solid State Physics, focusing on the synthesis and characterisation of Zinc Oxide (ZnO) nanostructures.

As a researcher, I have contributed to advanced research on Metamaterials characterisation under DMDE (DRDO) and published climate-impact correlation models in the Delhi University journal. In the classroom, I integrate ICT tools, Bloom's Taxonomy, and inquiry-led experiments to guide students from foundational wonder to analytical mastery.

I also serve as a Community Project Supervisor and Climate Action Project Supervisor, mentoring students to apply physical principles toward sustainable real-world solutions.
