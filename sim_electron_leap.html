Sub-Microscopic Redox Mechanism

Reactivity Series
  • Oxidation at the Solid Surface: Zinc sits higher on the reactivity series and has lower ionization energy. Zn loses its two valence electrons: Zn(s) → Zn²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻.
  • Reduction of Dissolved Cations: Aqueous Cu²⁺ ions accept these 2 electrons, discharging their characteristic blue color and depositing as reddish-brown solid copper: Cu²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻ → Cu(s).
  • Spectator Sulfate Ions: SO₄²⁻ ions maintain electroneutrality in solution without gaining or losing electrons.
Oxidation Half-Equation (OIL):
Zn(s) → Zn²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻
Reduction Half-Equation (RIG):
Cu²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻ → Cu(s)
